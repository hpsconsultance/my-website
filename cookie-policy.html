Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how HPS Consultance L.L.C-FZ may use cookies and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

1.1. Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They help websites function efficiently, remember user preferences, enhance security, and provide analytical insights to improve user experience.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

When you visit https://hpsconsultance.com/, we may use the following categories of cookies:

Cookie CategoryPurpose & FunctionalityConsent Required
Strictly Necessary CookiesEssential for core website functionality, security, network management, and contact form submissions. The site cannot operate properly without them.No (Essential)
Performance & Analytics CookiesCollect aggregated, anonymous statistics (pages visited, session duration, traffic sources) to help us optimize site speed and navigation.Yes
Functional CookiesRemember user preferences (such as language or regional settings) to deliver a tailored browsing experience.Yes

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

3.1. Cookie Consent Banner: Upon your first visit to https://hpsconsultance.com/, you may accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies through our cookie consent banner.

3.2. Browser Controls: You can also block, restrict, or delete cookies at any time by adjusting your web browser settings (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge). Please note that disabling strictly necessary or functional cookies may affect the availability or performance of certain features on our website.

4. Updates to This Cookie Policy

4.1. We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulatory requirements, or our website operations. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
