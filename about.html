ABOUT HPS

Practical support for the work behind your business.

A dependable partner for the day-to-day functions that keep your business moving.

WHO WE ARE

A partner behind your business

HPS Consultance L.L.C-FZ is a Dubai-based consultancy licensed by Meydan Free Zone. We support businesses with essential back-office functions across HR, employment coordination, finance coordination and IT support. Our role is to help bring these moving parts together, so business owners and teams can spend more time on their customers, people and growth.

Every business has different priorities. Some need help coordinating recruitment and employment processes; others need a reliable point of contact for HR administration, external finance professionals or everyday IT requirements. We take time to understand the requirement and help organise the right support around it.

HOW WE WORK

Our Approach

We start by understanding how your business operates, what support is already in place and where the gaps are. From there, we agree on the scope, responsibilities and a practical way of working. We coordinate with your internal team and relevant external providers, keeping communication clear and follow-ups organised.

Where a specialist service is required, we help coordinate with the appropriate qualified third-party provider. This gives you a clearer process and one point of contact without suggesting that every specialist service is delivered directly by HPS.

WHAT CLIENTS CAN EXPECT

A straightforward way to work

  • Clear scope: agreed responsibilities and practical next steps.
  • Consistent coordination: organised communication with your team and service providers.
  • Reliable follow-through: attention to routine tasks, timelines and pending actions.
  • Flexible support: an approach that can respond as your business needs change.

RESPONSIBLE BUSINESS

Our Commitment to Compliance

We are committed to conducting our business responsibly and in line with applicable UAE laws and regulatory requirements. We take a careful approach to client onboarding, due diligence and the handling of information. Where work requires regulated or specialist expertise, we coordinate with appropriately qualified external professionals. HPS does not provide audit, banking or investment services, and does not hold or manage client funds.
