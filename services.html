OUR SERVICES

Practical expertise for the work behind your business.

From people and employment coordination to finance support and IT, we bring essential business services together through one dependable point of contact.

HR Management

Recruitment and talent acquisition, onboarding and offboarding, employment contracts, HR policies, leave and attendance, and ongoing HR support for your team.

Hire in the UAE (Employer of Record)

For companies outside the UAE: we employ your chosen staff in the UAE on your behalf and handle contracts, visas, payroll and employment compliance.

Payroll & Visa Coordination

Monthly payroll processing support and coordination of visa and labour formalities with the relevant authorities.

Finance Coordination

We liaise with your accountants, auditors and VAT / corporate tax agents, collect and organise documents, and keep filing deadlines on track. Your providers are engaged and paid by you directly.

Budgeting & Management Reporting

Support with budgets, cost tracking and management reports to help you run the business.

IT Support & Consulting

IT consulting, systems and software set-up, and coordination with your IT vendors.

Management Consultancy

Organisational structure, processes and practical advice to make operations run smoothly.
