OUR SERVICES

Practical support for every stage of your business.

From people and employment coordination to finance support and IT, we bring essential business services together through one dependable point of contact.

IT Consultancy

We help businesses choose, plan and implement technology solutions that support their goals. From system setup to digital transformation, we help ensure your IT environment works efficiently and securely.

Finance & Tax Coordination

We coordinate with your appointed accounting and tax professionals to help organise financial records, VAT and Corporate Tax documentation, and compliance deadlines. Specialist accounting, tax advice and filings are handled by your appointed licensed providers.

Management Consultancy

We work with business owners and leaders to review operations, strategy and overall performance. Our practical guidance helps address business challenges, streamline processes and support sustainable growth.

HR Consultancy

We support businesses in developing effective HR practices and stronger teams. This includes HR policies, organisational structure, performance management and guidance on UAE labour law compliance.

Temporary Staffing Solutions

We help businesses source skilled professionals for short-term needs and project-based assignments. Whether you need extra support during a busy period or cover for a temporary gap, we coordinate flexible staffing solutions.

Hire in the UAE (Employer of Record)

For companies outside the UAE, we coordinate employment arrangements for your chosen staff in the UAE, including contracts, visa processes, payroll coordination and employment formalities.

Payroll & Visa Coordination

Monthly payroll support and coordination of visa and labour formalities with the relevant authorities and appointed service providers.
